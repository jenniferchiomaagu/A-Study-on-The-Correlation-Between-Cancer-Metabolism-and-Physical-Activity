🧬 A Study on the Correlation Between Cancer Metabolism and Physical Activity.

## Overview

This project explores the emerging field of exercise oncology , focusing on how different forms of physical activity influence cancer metabolism. Using the Recon3D metabolic model and Python-based simulations, I investigated the effects of resistance and endurance training on metabolic pathways relevant to cancer progression and recovery.

## Objective

Simulate metabolic activity under exercise conditions using Recon3D

Compare the impact of resistance vs endurance training on cancer-related metabolic functions

Explore potential mechanisms by which physical activity supports cancer treatment and recovery


Methodology

1. Literature review on exercise oncology and cancer metabolism
2. Python-based simulation using Recon3D model
3. Analysis of metabolic flux changes under different exercise conditions


Tools Used

1. Python
2. Recon3D metabolic model
3. Jupyter Notebook


Key Findings

1. Resistance and endurance training influence distinct metabolic pathways
2. Simulated data suggests exercise may support metabolic reprogramming in cancer contexts
3.  Further research needed to validate findings and explore potential exercise prescriptions to improve patient outcome
